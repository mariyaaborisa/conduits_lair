What we do

Cyborg Commons is a small consulting firm for movement-focused organizations facing high social engineering risk, including political leaders and small green firms.

  • Technology architecture and infrastructure design
  • Risk management
  • Data transformation
  • Digital security
  • Training
  • Ongoing support for your IT team

Why little tech

Little tech means tools small enough for a community to understand, govern, and own.

  • Small enough to understandYour team can see how it works.
  • Governed by your communityYou set the access and the terms.
  • Easy to take with youOpen formats and clean exports.
  • Built with the environment in mindLighter tools and hosting you can choose.

Big platforms are useful. We help you use them with open eyes and bring in little tech where it fits your work.
