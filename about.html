About

I'm Maria-Teresa C., a community technology engineer, and I run Cyborg Commons.

Cyborg Commons is community-level technology support that breaks down the walls of IT departments. It is woman-owned and neurodivergent-led. We help movement organizations, political leadership, and union community members revise their threat level as technology keeps changing.

I'm pro little tech and pro-environment, and I'm a mother, artist, and writer tinkering toward revolution.
